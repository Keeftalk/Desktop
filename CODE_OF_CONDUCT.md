# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free open-source community for everyone regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the Keeftalk community and user privacy.

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `mail@mourad.tn`.
